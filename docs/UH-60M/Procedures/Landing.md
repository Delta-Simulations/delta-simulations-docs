## Landing

- **Roll-on landing**
    
    A roll-on landing may be used
    when the helicopter will not sustain a hover or to avoid
    hovering in snow or dust

- __Slope landing__
    
    The tailwheel should be locked
    and the parking brake should be set. For slope landings
    and all ground operations, avoid using combinations of
    excessive cyclic and low collective settings.
