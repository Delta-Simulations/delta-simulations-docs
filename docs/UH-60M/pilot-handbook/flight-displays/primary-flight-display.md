# Primary Flight Display

Select **PFD** on the bottom bezel. Use the horizon in the center to read pitch and bank. The heading scale shows which way the nose is pointing. The airspeed indicator is on the left, with altitude and vertical speed on the right.

| Indication | How to read it |
| --- | --- |
| **KTS** | Indicated airspeed in knots. A headwind can give you airspeed while your position over the ground changes very little. |
| Heading | The boxed number shows current gyro heading in degrees. With the flight director active, **HDG** and its bug show the selected heading separately. |
| **FEET** | Indicated barometric altitude. The adjacent **IN** readout gives the pressure setting in inches of mercury. |
| Vertical speed | The scale extends above and below zero. Read direction from the pointer and the rate in feet per minute. The digital number shows the magnitude for both a climb and a descent. |

## Low-speed indication

The digital airspeed window bottoms out at **30**. It can still read 30 while the helicopter is sitting on the ramp. For a check in the simulator, watch the scenery or your movement on the navigation display to see how quickly you're traveling over the ground.

## Heading selection

Compare the current heading with the selected heading when reading the compass. For example, a current heading of 085 and a selected heading of 100 place the bug to the right of the nose. **FD1** appears when the flight director is active. The heading bug marks a reference and can move while the helicopter is stationary.
