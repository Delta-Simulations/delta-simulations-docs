# Parking Brake and Tailwheel Lock

Two controls matter when the helicopter is sitting on its wheels. The parking brake holds the main wheels. The tailwheel lock controls whether the tailwheel can swivel. You can have a locked tailwheel and still roll forward.

| Control | Where to look in the cockpit | What changes in MSFS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parking brake | Handle with the tooltip **SET/STOW PARKING BRAKE** | The handle follows the simulator's parking brake position. A click toggles the brake and plays an interior handle sound. |
| Tailwheel lock | Pushbutton with the tooltip **TAIL WHEEL** | The button toggles the simulator's tailwheel lock. Its locked and unlocked indicators follow that state. |

## Checking a controller binding

Start at a parking spot on level ground. With the helicopter stationary, click the parking brake handle once and note its position. Use your hardware parking brake command once. The handle should move back. If one press sets and immediately releases it, look for a second assignment on the same button or a controller sending a second toggle when you release the button.

Repeat the check with the **TAIL WHEEL** button and watch its indicator. Trying to judge the lock by turning the pedals while stationary can be misleading. The V5.2.4 flight model disables steering while stopped and sets the locked tailwheel's swivel range to zero.

## At the end of a flight

The project's [Landing checklist](../../Procedures/Landing.md) calls out both controls. The [Parking and Shutdown checklist](../../Procedures/Parking%20and%20Shutdown.md) includes the parking brake and wheel chocks as separate items. If you come back to the sim after opening a menu, check the handle itself before assuming the brake is set.
