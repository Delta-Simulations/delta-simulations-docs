# Departure, Cruise and Arrival

For a first circuit in the simulator, choose two nearby airports with an open, level landing area and use a clear-weather preset. Add both airports to the flight plan before entering the cockpit. Use a consistent fuel and payload load so that each circuit starts with roughly the same helicopter.

## Departure from the ramp

After the [engine start and runup](../../Procedures/Starting%20Engines.md), put the **PFD** on one screen and **EICAS** on the next. Check that your physical collective and pedals still move the intended cockpit controls. Before moving, identify which control releases the parking brake and check the area through the cockpit and exterior cameras. If the helicopter yaws or drifts before you intend to depart, set it back down and check wind, trim, assistance settings and axis assignments one at a time.

During the departure, use a fixed point outside for drift and glance at EICAS for rotor speed and the relationship between the two engine indications. As the helicopter accelerates, compare PFD indicated airspeed with the HUD **G** groundspeed. Wind can produce a difference on an otherwise steady departure.

## En route

Show **ND** on the adjacent screen when you want the route back. Confirm the next waypoint name and the direction of the leg. A turn in heading can precede a change in track, particularly in a crosswind. The line between the two airports remains fixed to the route.

Write down time and total fuel once established in cruise. Repeat the reading on the same units later in the leg. For example, if 12 NM remain and your actual groundspeed along the route is 80 kt, travel time is about nine minutes before any circuit or hover. The [fuel and endurance chapter](../flight-planning/fuel-load-and-cruise-endurance.md) shows how to apply your measured burn.

## Arrival and parking

Check destination elevation and the local pressure setting. At an elevated airport, barometric altitude can still read thousands of feet at touchdown while radar altitude reads near zero. On the last part of a visual approach, cross-check movement against the landing spot outside. The PFD airspeed window bottoms out at 30 knots, so it is a poor indication that all drift has stopped.

After touchdown, complete the [landing checklist](../../Procedures/Landing.md). Use [parking and shutdown](../../Procedures/Parking%20and%20Shutdown.md) when you reach the stand. If you want to repeat the circuit with different weather or a heavier load, change one of those conditions and retain your first set of readings for comparison.
