# H-60 Variant Selection

Several H-60s share the same aircraft type in the selector. Check the full variation name when choosing a helicopter. The two civilian S-70i entries, for example, carry the same registration but load different equipment.

## Included utility and rescue aircraft

These are selection labels from the V5.2.4 package. The **HOIST** and **PROBE** columns show which equipment controls the model enables on the cockpit tablet.

| Variation | How to recognize it | HOIST | PROBE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **UH-60M 20743** | Standard utility Mike with upturned exhausts | — | — |
| **UH-60M 20743 (HIRSS)** | Alternate exhaust installation on the Mike | — | — |
| **HH-60M Medevac 20946** or **HH-60M Medevac 21018** | Medical cabin and external rescue hoist | Available | — |
| **HH-60U Ghost Hawk 20321** | HH-60U model | Available | — |

| **S70i Civilian Helitak N60HD** | Civilian model with a fixed belly tank | — | — |

## Check the equipment after spawning

Open the cockpit tablet's **Aircraft** tab. On an HH-60M Medevac, **HOIST** is available and **PROBE** is dimmed. On the Helitak, **WATER SYSTEM** identifies the fixed tank as **HELITAK**. If you loaded an S-70i and see **NO WATER EQUIPMENT**, check the full variant name before changing the aircraft configuration.

For a medical flight, look into the cabin as well as at the paint. The HH-60M uses a dedicated patient interior and has a sealed section at the crew window. A repaint can look like a rescue helicopter while still using the base utility model. To check whether an unexpected cabin or disabled tablet control comes from the repaint, load one of the stock entries in the table and compare.
