# Cockpit Tablet and Map

The H-60 carries its own cockpit tablet. Click **PEDAL ADJUST** to hide or show it. With cockpit tooltips enabled, the click spot reads **EFB HIDE/SHOW**. Use the tabs along the bottom edge of the tablet to change pages.

| Tab | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Aircraft** | Crew and door controls, equipment for the selected variant, the water system, and external light controls. |
| **Map** | An embedded OpenTopoMap. It needs an internet connection to load the map tiles. |
| **Settings** | Rotor appearance, custom downwash effects, tablet brightness, and a reference list of hardware bindings. |

## Map behavior

The map opens at a preset location in Kentucky. An aircraft spawned elsewhere can therefore be hundreds of miles from the area on screen. Pan and zoom to explore the area you want. For aircraft navigation, use the navigation display in the cockpit. The tablet map opens an external map location independently of the aircraft position and active flight plan.

If only **Map** is blank, switch back to **Aircraft**. Working door and equipment controls indicate that the tablet itself is running. Check internet access to the map provider before reinstalling the H-60.

## Screen and rotor settings

On **Settings**, the **EFB BRIGHTNESS** slider runs from 0% to 100%. Change it here if the tablet is hard to read. If the mount and tablet have disappeared together, use the **PEDAL ADJUST** click spot first.

Under **VISUAL CONFIGURATION**, **ROTOR TYPE** switches between **MESH** and **BLURRED**. Change the setting and compare the rotor from the same external camera angle. **CUSTOM DOWNWASH FX** has its own on/off setting, so you can compare the two rotor styles without changing the downwash effect.

The keybindings listed on **Settings** are a reference. To assign one, open the controls menu in MSFS, select the hardware device you intend to use, and edit its active profile.
