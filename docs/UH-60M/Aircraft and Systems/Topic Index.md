## Finding a cockpit indication

Use the label beside the reading to choose a page. **NR** is [rotor speed](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/engine-and-rotor-indications.md), **RA** is [height above terrain](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/barometric-and-radar-altitude.md), and **REF** identifies the [selected display reference](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/flight-director-and-mode-selectors.md). For a message that appeared during a test, look up its exact wording under [crew alerts](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/crew-alerts-and-test-indications.md).
