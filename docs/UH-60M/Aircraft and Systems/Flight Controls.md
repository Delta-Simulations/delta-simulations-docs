# Cyclic, Collective and Pedals

Set up these axes in your MSFS helicopter controls profile. The pilot and copilot have the same primary controls.

| Cockpit control | Axis | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cyclic | Pitch and roll | Tilts the rotor disc forward, aft, left or right. |
| Collective | Collective | Changes the pitch of all main rotor blades together. |
| Pedals | Yaw | Changes tail rotor thrust to swing the nose left or right. |

Move one control at a time and watch the matching control in the cockpit. If the nose turns the wrong way when you press a pedal, check the direction of the yaw axis in your profile.

**SAS** is the stability augmentation system. It damps unwanted motion. You'll also see **TRIM** and **FPS** (flight path stabilization) in the [cockpit equipment checks](../Procedures/Cockpit%20Equipment%20Checks.md).

## Setup and flight guidance

Assign cyclic pitch and roll, collective, and a single yaw axis on your [helicopter controller profile](../pilot-handbook/controls-and-lighting/helicopter-controller-profiles.md). If the cockpit pedals jump back as soon as you release them, check for another yaw assignment on a twist stick or gamepad. To isolate that input before changing sensitivity, use [axis calibration and troubleshooting](../pilot-handbook/controls-and-lighting/axis-calibration-and-troubleshooting.md).

[SAS, FPS and rotor trim](../pilot-handbook/controls-and-lighting/rotor-trim-and-flight-assistance.md) affect a different part of the control setup from [flight director and mode selection](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/flight-director-and-mode-selectors.md). The PFD can display a director command while the pilot still flies the maneuver.
