# Multifunction Display Pages

Use the bottom bezel to choose what appears on each screen. A screen set to PFD can stay in that format while another shows the route or engine indications. The V5.2.4 bezel and older checklist captions differ in two places. The data page is labeled **JMVF** on the bezel and its load key reads **LOAD DTS** on screen.

| Page | Selection | What appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PFD** | Press **PFD**. | Attitude, heading, indicated airspeed, altitude and vertical speed. [PFD indications](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/primary-flight-display.md) |
| **ND** | Press **ND**. | Compass and simulator flight-plan waypoints. [Navigation display](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/navigation-display-and-flight-plan.md) |
| **EICAS** | Press **EICAS**. | Engine and rotor indications, fuel quantities, cautions and advisories. [Engine instruments](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/engine-and-rotor-indications.md) |
| **TAC** | Press **TAC**. | Moving map with scale, orientation and centering controls. [Moving map](../pilot-handbook/communications-and-navigation/moving-map-and-route-entry.md) |
| **JMVF** | Press **JMVF**. | Waypoint-data load key and DTS status. [Data transfer](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/data-transfer-and-aircraft-status.md) |
| **IVHMS** | From EICAS, press **EICAS** again. | Administration page with aircraft identification, version and DTS status. Press **EICAS** again to return. [Aircraft status](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/data-transfer-and-aircraft-status.md) |

If the bezel lettering is visible but the screen is dark, check that display's **PWR** switch and the shared backlight. See [display power and bezel keys](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/display-power-and-bezel-keys.md).
