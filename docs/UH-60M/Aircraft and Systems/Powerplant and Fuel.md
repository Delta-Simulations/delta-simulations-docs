# Powerplant and Fuel

!!! warning "For simulation only"
    This page describes systems represented in Microsoft Flight Simulator. It is not a real-world operating procedure or a substitute for an approved aircraft manual.

## Engines

The UH-60M uses two T700-series turboshaft engines. Each has its own controls and indications. Engine and rotor information is presented through the cockpit engine display.

The add-on’s engine behavior, start logic, and indications depend on the modeled systems in the installed version. Follow the simulator [Starting Engines](../Procedures/Starting%20Engines.md) page for the current add-on flow.

## Fuel

The aircraft has two main fuel tanks and selectable engine-feed arrangements. Fuel quantity and system status are shown through cockpit indications. The source manual describes separate engine fuel paths and boost-pump equipment used for priming and APU support.

Use the cockpit controls and simulator fuel menu as modeled. The add-on may simplify or omit real-aircraft fuel-system functions.

## Auxiliary Power Unit

The APU provides electrical and pneumatic support for ground operations and engine start. Its switches and indications are on the overhead and cockpit displays.
