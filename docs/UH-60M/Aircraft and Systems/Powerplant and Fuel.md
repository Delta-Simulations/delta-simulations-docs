# Engines, Fuel and APU

The UH-60M has two T700-GE-701D turboshaft engines. Select **EICAS** on a multifunction display to see the engine and rotor indications together.

## Fuel system

The helicopter carries fuel in two main tanks. Each engine has its own fuel feed. On the overhead panel, the engine boost pump switches are separate from the **FUEL PUMP** selector used for APU boost.

## Auxiliary power unit

The APU supplies electrical power on the ground and bleed air for engine start. The **APU CONT** switch controls the unit. The APU generator switch controls its electrical output.

For the switch sequence in the sim, see [Starting Engines](../Procedures/Starting%20Engines.md).

## Read the panel during a start

Look for **APU ON** and **APU GEN ON** separately. A running APU can supply bleed air with its generator disconnected. Follow the [air-source and ground-power controls](../pilot-handbook/power-and-fuel/bleed-air-and-ground-power.md) when one source appears available but another does not.

Use the [power control lever guide](../pilot-handbook/power-and-fuel/engine-power-control-levers.md) for the modeled detents. Compare [Q, NG, TGT, NP and NR](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/engine-and-rotor-indications.md) by label. The PFD's two torque indications use separate engine inputs, while both sets of V5.2.4 EICAS engine tapes read engine 1.

For a fuel check, read the quantities in both [main tanks](../pilot-handbook/power-and-fuel/main-fuel-tanks-and-boost-pumps.md) before and after a timed leg. Use total fuel consumed by both engines for [cruise endurance](../pilot-handbook/flight-planning/fuel-load-and-cruise-endurance.md).
