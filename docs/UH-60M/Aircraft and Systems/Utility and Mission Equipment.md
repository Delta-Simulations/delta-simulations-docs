# Cabin, Hoist and Utility Equipment

Cabin equipment depends on which H-60 you select in MSFS.

| Variant | Equipment |
| --- | --- |
| **UH-60M “Mike”** | Utility cabin for passengers and cargo. |
| **HH-60M “Dustoff”** | Medical cabin with a patient treatment system and an external rescue hoist. |
| **S-70i “Helitak”** | Firefighting tank. The [Keybinds Guide](../Keybinds_Guide.md) lists its release binding under **Bambi/Helitak Release**. |

The [Variants page](Simulated_Variants.md) has photos of the different configurations. The aircraft manual also covers the external cargo hook. Check your installed version for interactive hook and hoist support.

## Configuring a rescue or firefighting flight

Start with the full aircraft title in the [variant guide](../pilot-handbook/installation-and-equipment/h-60-variant-selection.md). A repaint can supply a medical paint scheme while retaining the utility cabin. On a stock HH-60M Medevac, open **Aircraft** on the tablet and look at the hoist and door controls. The [equipment guide](../pilot-handbook/installation-and-equipment/doors-crew-and-external-equipment.md) identifies the moving doors, crew visibility switches and variant-specific fittings.

On the S-70i Helitak, check the water-system indication before assigning a release control. The [firefighting equipment guide](../pilot-handbook/installation-and-equipment/bambi-bucket-and-helitak-tank.md) distinguishes the fixed belly tank from the bucket. Set passenger and cargo weights in [MSFS payload stations](../pilot-handbook/flight-planning/gross-weight-and-payload-stations.md) separately from the visible cabin configuration.
