# Electrical and Hydraulic Systems

!!! warning "For simulation only"
    This is a systems overview for the Delta Simulations add-on, not maintenance guidance or real-aircraft operating instruction.

## Electrical power

The aircraft uses batteries, engine-driven generators, and an APU generator to provide electrical power. The cockpit includes battery and generator controls, electrical indications, and circuit-breaker panels.

For the simulator, follow the modeled start-up and shutdown procedures. Bus behavior, power transfers, and failure indications may be simplified.

## Hydraulic assistance

Hydraulic systems provide powered assistance to the flight controls and other aircraft functions. The cockpit includes controls and indications for the primary and backup hydraulic equipment.

The add-on may not model every hydraulic-system feature or failure described in the aircraft manual. Use the current add-on release notes for implementation details.
