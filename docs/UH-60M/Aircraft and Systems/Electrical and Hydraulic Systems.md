# Electrical Power and Hydraulics

## Batteries and generators

The batteries provide power before a generator is online. On the overhead panel, **BATT NO. 1** and **BATT NO. 2** control the batteries. The two engine generators and the APU generator each have a separate switch.

## Flight control hydraulics

Hydraulic pressure assists the flight controls. The **BACKUP HYD PUMP** switch belongs to the hydraulic system. The nearby fuel pump switches serve the engines and APU.

The [Before Start checklist](../Procedures/Before_Start.md) gives the initial switch positions. The [Cockpit Equipment Checks](../Procedures/Cockpit%20Equipment%20Checks.md) cover the next part of the startup.

## When a panel goes dark

Start with the extent of the problem. One dark MFD with a working neighbor points to that screen's power selection. Several dark displays and silent radios call for a look at the main electrical supply. The [electrical bus and generator guide](../pilot-handbook/power-and-fuel/batteries-generators-and-display-power.md) identifies the main, hot battery and essential avionics circuits. The [standby instrument](../pilot-handbook/airframe-and-instruments/standby-flight-instruments.md) has its own battery selection.

## When several hydraulic messages appear together

The parked leak test drives a group of reservoir, pump and servo messages from one test state. Moving the switch from **TEST** back to **NORM** leaves the state latched in V5.2.4. The [hydraulic test guide](../pilot-handbook/power-and-fuel/hydraulic-leak-test-indications.md) covers **RESET** and lists the expected messages by area of the screen. Compare any remaining message with the [EICAS caution list](../pilot-handbook/flight-displays/crew-alerts-and-test-indications.md).
