# Electrical Power and Hydraulics

## Batteries and generators

The batteries provide power before a generator is online. On the overhead panel, **BATT NO. 1** and **BATT NO. 2** control the batteries. The two engine generators and the APU generator each have a separate switch.

## Flight control hydraulics

Hydraulic pressure assists the flight controls. The **BACKUP HYD PUMP** switch belongs to the hydraulic system. The nearby fuel pump switches serve the engines and APU.

The [Before Start checklist](../Procedures/Before_Start.md) gives the initial switch positions. The [Cockpit Equipment Checks](../Procedures/Cockpit%20Equipment%20Checks.md) cover the next part of the startup.
