# Electrical and Hydraulic Systems

!!! warning "Simulation use only"
    This is a systems overview for the Delta Simulations add-on, not maintenance guidance or real-aircraft operating instruction.

## Electrical power

The aircraft's electrical system uses multiple batteries, engine-driven generators, and an APU generator to provide power to aircraft equipment. The cockpit includes battery and generator controls, electrical indications, and circuit-breaker panels.

For the simulator, follow the modeled start-up and shutdown flow in [Normal Procedures](../Operations.md). The add-on may simplify bus behavior, power transfers, or failure indications.

## Hydraulic assistance

Hydraulic systems provide powered assistance to the flight controls and other aircraft functions. The cockpit includes controls and indications for the primary and backup hydraulic equipment.

The simulation may not model all hydraulic-system details or failures described in the aircraft manual. Use the add-on's current feature notes for the implemented behavior.
