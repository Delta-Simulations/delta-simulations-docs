# Powerplant and Fuel

!!! warning "Simulation use only"
    This page describes modeled systems for Microsoft Flight Simulator. It is not a real-world operating procedure or a substitute for an approved aircraft manual.

## Engines

The UH-60M uses two T700-series turboshaft engines. Each engine has its own controls and indications. The flight deck presents engine and rotor information through the Engine Instrument Caution/Advisory System (EICAS).

The add-on's engine behavior, start logic, and indications are limited to the systems modeled in the installed version. Use the add-on's [Normal Procedures](../Operations.md) page for its current simulation flow.

## Fuel system

The aircraft has two main fuel tanks and selectable engine feed arrangements. Fuel quantity and system status are represented through cockpit indications. The manual describes separate engine fuel supply paths and the use of boost-pump equipment for priming and APU support.

For this add-on, use the cockpit controls and simulator fuel menu as modeled. Do not assume every real-aircraft fuel-system feature is implemented.

## Auxiliary Power Unit

The APU is an onboard source of electrical and pneumatic support used during ground operations and engine start. Its switches and indications are on the overhead and cockpit displays.
