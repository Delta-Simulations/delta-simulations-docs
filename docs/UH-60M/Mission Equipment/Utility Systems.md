# Utility and Mission Equipment

!!! warning "Simulation use only"
    This overview is for the Delta Simulations add-on. It does not provide real-world loading, rigging, rescue, medical, or mission procedures.

The UH-60M family can be configured with utility equipment for transport and support tasks. Depending on the aircraft variant and add-on configuration, the simulation may include:

- Cabin and cargo-area equipment
- External cargo-hook controls
- Rescue-hoist controls
- Medical-evacuation interior options
- Auxiliary equipment controlled from cockpit panels

The aircraft manual covers these systems in separate sections. The add-on may represent only selected equipment, and some controls may be visual or nonfunctional. Check the aircraft's in-simulator EFB and current release notes for the features available in your version.
